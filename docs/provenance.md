# Provenance and Attribution

The application grew from creator-supplied mathematical exploration of PRISM India problems. The owner identifies Radhe Dhoundiyal as the sole human contributor. Software, later audits, corrections and public-edition preparation were AI-assisted. New editorial introductions and later corrections are not contemporaneous notebook reasoning; the software license grants only rights held by the owner.

Authenticated originals remain private and unchanged: seven notebook PDFs, 72 page renders and seven historical TeX solutions. Their original SHA-256 values are never reassigned to derivatives. The complete official question paper and Problem 8 statement-only extract are not included in the research package.

The official external reference is [PRISM India](https://www.prism-india.org/). This is an unofficial project with no organizer endorsement. No third-party content is automatically covered by the software license.

Public editions are separate edited documents. They retain source order and numbering, label omissions, remove identified statement passages/recreated organizer figures and clean unnecessary PDF metadata. Sixty page slots retain the full reasoning image, eight retain selected regions, and four use explicitly new editorial settings instead of statement-dominated images. Every image and PDF is labeled; no edited page is described as an exact original replica. All seven TeX editions identify their new setting; historical conclusions remain unchanged, apart from a labeled omission of quoted task wording in Problem 7.

The public-edition manifest in `src/research/public-editions.json` records source/derivative hashes and changes. The separately prepared `research/v1/manifest.json` pins the asset files, which are not included here or publicly deployed. Research publication rights are not inferred from a successful content scan. See [research packaging](research-availability.md) and [license scope](licensing.md).

This is a curated successor, not the unchanged historical repository. The private history and complete reference edition are preserved; no fictional public development history has been created.
