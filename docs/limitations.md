# Limitations

- Binary search and bit-width inputs are capped at 512.
- Parity simulations are capped at 10,000 steps, 10,000 trials and five million transitions. Displayed rounding does not make floating-point results exact.
- Number Forge uses finite DP through 10,000, at most twelve generators and at most 128 residue classes. A failed sufficient certificate does not establish impossibility.
- Grid exact prediction requires at most ten empty cells; larger Hard play uses a depth-four heuristic. Independent larger-board checks are partial.
- Language enumeration is complete only through length 10. User explanations are recorded, not mechanically proved. A witness confirms an existence claim; it refutes a universal prohibition only when that is the proposition being tested.
- Temporal editing allows at most 40 nodes and 100 edges, distinct positive integer edge times and simple undirected pairs. Abstract drawing distances are not a unit-lattice certificate.
- Loop exact BFS is capped at n=7 and 6,000 states; other tools have separate limits. Bounds cannot be overridden by public caller options.
- Blackboard exact initial admission requires at most five entries and sum at most 30, with unchanged 50,000-expansion/depth-256 limits. Truncated graphs do not enumerate all terminal possibilities.
- localStorage may be unavailable, full or corrupt. Import/reset rollback is best effort, not atomic across tabs or crashes. Keep recovery exports.
- Automated axe, keyboard and responsive checks do not establish full WCAG conformance or replace manual assistive-technology review.
- Public research editions omit identified statement material and retain historical mistakes with separate corrections. Page images are not complete screen-reader transcriptions. Untouched originals remain private; Problem 8's missing notebook has not been invented or replaced.
- MIT covers eligible software rights held by Radhe Dhoundiyal, not separately reserved research or third-party material. The approved public research package is hosted separately on Vercel Blob. Publication does not grant new reuse rights or authorize held originals; future editions require separate authorization. Local checks do not establish legal clearance, remote CI or deployment success.
